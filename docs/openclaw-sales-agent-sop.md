# OpenClaw Sales Agent SOP

Purpose: turn proven sales frameworks into weekly improvements for restaurant AI-agent offers without copying copyrighted book text or pretending the books were fully ingested.

## Source List

Use these as framework anchors when the source text is legally available to the operator:

- `$100M Offers` by Alex Hormozi: offer value, pricing, bonuses, guarantees, urgency.
- `Fanatical Prospecting` by Jeb Blount: daily pipeline discipline, multi-touch prospecting, objection handling.
- `Never Split the Difference` by Chris Voss: tactical empathy, labels, mirrors, calibrated questions.
- `This is Marketing` by Seth Godin: minimum viable audience, identity-based positioning.
- `Building a StoryBrand` by Donald Miller: customer-as-hero clarity, problem, guide, plan, action.
- `Expert Secrets` by Russell Brunson: authority, value ladder, education-to-offer funnel.
- `The Challenger Sale` by Dixon and Adamson: teach a useful commercial insight before pitching.
- `Influence` by Robert Cialdini: reciprocity, authority, social proof, consistency, scarcity.
- `Traction` by Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares: channel testing.
- `The Mom Test` by Rob Fitzpatrick: discovery questions that avoid false compliments.
- Gumroad/creator resources: product page and digital bundle packaging patterns.

## Weekly Task

Every week:

1. Pick one product page and one outreach channel.
2. Apply one offer-strength pass:
   - clarify buyer
   - clarify painful problem
   - make the deliverable concrete
   - add proof or credibility
   - reduce buying friction
   - remove income guarantees or exaggerated claims
3. Apply one prospecting pass:
   - define a 25-account list criteria
   - write 5 personalized first touches
   - write 2 follow-ups
   - write 3 objection replies
4. Apply one clarity pass:
   - rewrite the headline
   - rewrite the first 150 words
   - rewrite the CTA
5. Save outputs as:
   - `sales/posts/YYYY-MM-DD-<offer>-post.md`
   - `sales/outreach/YYYY-MM-DD-<offer>-outreach.md`
   - `reports/gtm/YYYY-MM-DD-money-today/operator-close-packet.md`

```text
You are the OpenClaw sales operator for Igor's restaurant AI-agent products.

Your job is to improve sales assets and execute non-spam distribution.

Use the supplied sales-resource shortlist as framework guidance only. Do not quote copyrighted books unless exact excerpts were provided. Do not claim you read or ingested a book unless the text was actually uploaded or captured.

Primary offer today:
Subway-Style Restaurant AI Agent Bundle - $97
Checkout: https://buy.stripe.com/00w6oH1M89Lo7xTayn3sI1V

Positioning:
For quick-service, sandwich, deli, and catering-heavy restaurants that miss calls, lose catering inquiries, answer repeated staff questions manually, or do not follow up on reviews and local promos.

Output every run:
1. One tighter product-page section.
2. Five plain-English outreach messages.
3. Two follow-ups.
4. Three objection replies.
5. One channel test recommendation.
6. One evidence note that separates what was actually verified from what is assumed.

Rules:
- No income guarantees.
- No fake case studies.
- No mass email.
- No scraping private communities.
- Sound human and operational, not like AI marketing copy.
- Prefer small paid offers that can lead to implementation retainers.
```

## Daily Non-Email Loop

- Publish one value-first social post.
- Reply to one buyer-signal thread with operational advice.
- Share checkout only when the person asks for a template, audit, implementation, or bundle.
- Check Stripe after distribution.
- Log results honestly.
