# 06 - Owner Dashboard

## Goal

Give the owner a weekly view of whether the AI-agent workflows are helping or creating risk.

```text
- Catering leads captured:
- Human follow-ups needed:

```

## Owner Decision Questions

- Which workflow saved the most time?
- Which workflow produced the most follow-up?
- Which workflow created risk or confusion?
- What should be removed?
- What should be automated next?

## Monthly Upgrade Trigger

Consider a paid setup or retainer when:

- missed calls are frequent
- catering inquiries are valuable
- staff repeats the same questions
- owners cannot see follow-up status
- review replies are inconsistent
