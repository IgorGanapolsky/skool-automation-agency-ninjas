# 07 - Implementation Checklist

## Before Launch

- Store name is correct.
- Hours are correct.
- Address and phone are correct.
- Catering availability is correct.
- Escalation contacts are listed.
- Refund, allergy, HR, and complaint limits are clear.
- Agent has been tested with 10 realistic examples.
- Owner knows where summaries appear.

## Launch Order

1. Staff FAQ agent.
2. Review response drafts.
3. Local promo drafts.
4. Catering intake.
5. Missed-call capture.

The safest workflows are internal or draft-only. Put customer-facing automation last.

## Test Cases

- Customer asks for catering for 40 people tomorrow.
- Customer asks whether a sandwich is allergen-free.
- Customer complains about a bad order.
- Staff asks closing checklist question.
- Customer asks for a refund.
- Office manager asks about weekly lunch orders.
- Review says the service was slow but the food was good.

## Human Handoff Rule

Escalate when the request involves:

- allergies
- injury or food safety
- refund or chargeback
- employee complaint
- harassment or discrimination
- legal threat
- unclear pricing
- angry customer
- anything the agent cannot answer from approved material

## Go-Live Checklist

- Start with one workflow.
- Review transcripts daily for the first week.
- Update unknown answers.
- Track follow-ups.
- Remove any answer that feels risky.
- Keep owner approval for policy-sensitive changes.
